May, 23, 2024

Ph.D. student Dongha Chung was awarded the grand prize from KAOSTS Paper Contest A paper entitled “Pohang canal dataset: A multimodal maritime dataset for autonomous navigation in restricted waters” which was published in IJRR (https://doi.org/10.1177/02783649231191145) was awarded as the grand prize from Korean Assoication of Ocean Science and Technology Societies (KAOSTS) Paper Contest (Future Ocean Scientist and Engineer Award) held under the joint auspices of the Ministry of Oceans and Fisheries and KAOSTS.

Ph.D. student Qianyi Zhang was selected to participate in OCEANS’24 Student Poster Competition. A paper entitled “Feature-based Global Localization for Underwater Terrain Aided Navigation using Bag of Words” was selected to participate in the MTS/IEEE OCEANS’24 Student Poster Competition held in Singapore, Singapore, April 14- 18. February, 2024

Team KAIST awarded 2nd Place Winner in MBZIRC 2023 Team KAIST, led by students from the labs MORIN (of Prof. Jinhwan Kim) and USRG (of Prof. Hyunchul Shim), and with PABLO AIR as a partner, won the 2nd Place Winner position in the MBZIRC Maritime Grand Challenge, an international robot competition. Total 18 students from the aforementioned labs were led by the student leaders Sol Han and Haggi Do of the MORIN lab to achieve the automated mission accomplishment via USV-UAV integrated system in maritime environment.
