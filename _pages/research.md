MORIN Lab studies autonomy and intelligence for mobile robotic systems, with a strong emphasis on marine applications. The current website content is organized around the following research themes; this page is intentionally kept concise in this first migration and can be redesigned later.

## Current research themes

### Model Predictive Control

Control and motion-planning methods for autonomous robotic vehicles, including marine systems.

### Backward Reachability Analysis

Safety-oriented analysis and planning methods for autonomous systems.

### Radar-based Coastal Navigation

Navigation and localization using marine radar in coastal and GNSS-restricted environments.

### Multi-modal Target Association

Association and fusion of measurements from multiple sensing modalities for maritime perception and tracking.

### UUV Bathymetric SLAM

Simultaneous localization and mapping using underwater terrain and bathymetric information.

### Automated Mission Planning for Unmanned Vehicles

Mission and task planning for autonomous and cooperative unmanned systems, including autonomous marine systems.
