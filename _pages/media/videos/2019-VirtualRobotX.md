The Virtual RobotX (VRX) Competition is an international university-level competition conducted in a Gazebo-based simulation environment developed by Open Robotics and the Naval Postgraduate School. Participating students are challenged to develop innovative solutions that enable their virtual USVs to complete a series of prescribed autonomous tasks. More information and the final competition results are available on the [official RobotX website](https://robotx.org/2019/12/06/2019-virtual-robotx-competition-final-standings/).
